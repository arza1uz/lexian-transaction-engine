# Roadmap

This roadmap assumes about 2 hours per day, Monday to Friday, or roughly 10 focused hours per week. It is business-driven: new technology appears only when the Lexian product story needs it.

## Months 1-2: Foundation And Transaction Engine

Build the batch transaction pipeline, validation rules, tests, CI, and documentation. Make the first repository recruiter-friendly and easy to run.

## Month 3: Reconciliation

Add a separate reconciliation product that compares internal transaction records against partner or bank settlement files.

## Month 4: Risk And Fraud

Define early risk and fraud signals based on transaction behavior, data quality, and suspicious patterns.

## Month 5: Data Platform

Introduce local analytical storage, dimensional modeling, and clear data contracts for product analytics.

## Month 6: Orchestration And APIs

Add scheduled workflows and simple service interfaces when manual command execution becomes limiting.

## Month 7: Streaming And Distributed Processing

Explore event-driven processing when near-real-time balances, alerts, or fraud signals become business requirements.

## Month 8: Cloud And Infrastructure

Package services for deployment, add environment management, and document infrastructure decisions.

## Month 9: ML Platform

Build repeatable model training and evaluation workflows for risk, fraud, or credit use cases.

## Month 10: AI Platform

Experiment with responsible AI assistants, retrieval, and agent workflows for support or operations.

## Months 11-12: Observability, Security, Cost, And Polish

Improve monitoring, access controls, cost awareness, documentation quality, and portfolio presentation.
